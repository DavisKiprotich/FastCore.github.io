Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

FastCore is a local-first wellness tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and which service providers receive limited subscription data.

1. Information stored on your device

FastCore stores your display name, language, onboarding answers, fasting sessions and active-fast state, mood selections, hydration logs, weight entries, goals, and schedules locally on your device. FastCore does not require a FastCore account and does not upload this wellness history to a FastCore cloud database.

2. Subscription and technical information

Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment details. RevenueCat helps validate purchases and restore premium access. It may receive an anonymous app user ID, device type and operating system, locale and currency, last-seen time, product and entitlement information, Apple receipts, or Google purchase tokens. FastCore does not send your wellness records or display name to RevenueCat, and does not store your payment-card details.

3. How information is used

Local information is used to provide the timer, progress statistics, hydration and weight tracking, reminders, personalization, and backup and restore. Subscription information is used to show available plans, determine premium access, validate and restore purchases, prevent purchase fraud, and provide subscription support. FastCore does not sell personal information and does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. Backups and notifications

If device backup is enabled, Apple, Google, or your device provider may back up eligible app data under your settings and their policies. A backup you export is a readable JSON file and is not encrypted by FastCore; you choose where to save or share it and must protect it. Imported files are read only after you select them. With permission, FastCore schedules notifications locally on your device; notification text may appear on the lock screen according to device settings.

5. Sharing and international processing

Information is disclosed only as needed to Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Expo or operating-system services, and any storage provider you choose for a backup. These providers process information under their own terms and may do so in other countries. FastCore may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect users, the app, or legal rights.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local records remain until you reset FastCore, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Exported and device backups remain where you saved them until you delete them. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat retain purchase records under their own policies and legal duties. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription. You can manage notification permission and device backup in system settings and manage billing in the applicable app store.

7. Security, children, and contact

FastCore uses reasonable safeguards, but no device, file, or transmission method is completely secure. The app is not directed to children who cannot legally consent to its use, and minors should not fast without guidance from a guardian and qualified health professional. We may update this policy and will change the date above. For privacy questions or requests, contact devmile45@gmail.com.
