Subscriptions and billing

Weekly and annual subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. If a purchase is not recognized, use Restore Purchases on the paywall. In Profile, use Manage Subscription to open the billing store and cancel, change, or resubscribe. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Manage Apple subscriptionsManage Google Play subscriptions

Your data and backups

Fasting, water, and weight records are stored on your device. In Profile, open Backup & Restore to export a backup or import one you saved. Exported JSON files are readable and should be kept private. FastCore does not automatically sync this history between devices. On iPhone, Automatic iCloud Backup updates one file in FastCore’s iCloud Drive folder and keeps one local copy. In Backup & Restore you can restore the latest automatic backup. On Android, automatic backups stay local; Google Drive requires a manual export. Background timing and iCloud sync are not guaranteed at an exact time.

Accounts and health questions

The current FastCore app does not require an account or login. FastCore is a wellness tracking tool, not a medical service. For medical questions or symptoms, contact a qualified health professional.
